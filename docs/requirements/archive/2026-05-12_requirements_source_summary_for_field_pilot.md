# Source Requirements Summary For Field Pilot

## Summary Of Preserved Intent

The original product is an amateur basketball operations ecosystem, not only a
scoreboard UI. It includes game operation, smart scoreboard, digital
scorekeeping, team/player/league/tournament/gym data management, community,
matching, team intranet, PDF automation, hardware/display integration, and
future payment/credit/reservation/ad capabilities.

## Phase 1 Intent Preserved

Phase 1 is a free MVP/open beta focused on field usability:

- Scoreboard control panel
- Digital score sheet
- Dedicated scoreboard display route
- Game result and box score view
- Team, player, league, tournament, and game result CRUD
- Community basics
- Team intranet basics
- PDF template download, export, and basic parsing
- Guest, user, team manager, and system administrator roles
- Logging, validation, error handling, responsive UI, and tests

## Phase 2 Intent Preserved But Excluded From Pilot

Phase 2 remains future commercial expansion and is not part of the field pilot:

- Gym reservation payment
- Participation fee payment
- Credit recharge and wallet
- Points and settlement
- Ads
- Phone, email, bank account, and card verification
- Kakao AlimTalk, SMS, and email notifications
- Reviews, manner score, escrow-like policy, and no-show policy

## Field Pilot Relationship

The field pilot document narrows Phase 1 to a usable first deployment target for
one real amateur club or practice game. It does not delete or replace the larger
product requirements. Any future change that removes or narrows the original
requirements must be explicitly approved and recorded in a new dated document.
